Regional Airport Terminal Design

Structural design, class project

A seven gate regional terminal sized to support Aircraft Design Group Code E aircraft, including wide body types up to the Boeing 777 and Airbus A350. The continuous curved roof form ties ticketing, security, and gate areas into one structural envelope.

The terminal's space program totals roughly 45,000 sq. ft. across ticketing, security screening, baggage handling, concessions, and circulation. Structural spans were sized to keep the roof framing clear of the open concourse below.

Overview

This class project developed a fully functional small regional airport terminal designed to FAA standards, placing first in competition. The seven gate terminal supports Code E aircraft operations, with the space program, gate layout, and structural roof form developed together as one coordinated system rather than separate design pieces.
