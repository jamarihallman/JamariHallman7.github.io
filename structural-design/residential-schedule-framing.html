Project Overview

Developed a coordinated structural model and a 66-day construction schedule for a 26' × 30' two-story residential building on a 30' × 40' lot in Charlotte, NC, integrating design drawings, site plan, and floor plans.

Key Highlights

  • Created 66-day construction schedule
  • Integrated site, structure, and sequencing
